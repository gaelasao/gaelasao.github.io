Ireland planning applications

This page sums them up from the official open data (Department of Housing, Local Government and Heritage, Ireland — National Planning Application Database) and shows how to get the full list, record by record.

About this data

Source: Ireland — national planning applications (all planning authorities). Published by Department of Housing, Local Government and Heritage, Ireland — National Planning Application Database, updated daily (publisher). Licence: CC BY 4.0. Values are the amounts declared on the application, not final costs.

  • Ireland: applications sent by every planning authority to the national database; coverage and delays follow each authority.
  • Applicant names and addresses are not collected.
  • No project value. Floor and site areas are as declared.

Questions

How often is Ireland application data updated?

Department of Housing, Local Government and Heritage, Ireland — National Planning Application Database updates the open data daily (publisher). A run always reads the latest published records.

Can I get only some planning applications?

Yes. Filter by date range, keyword in the description or type, and cap the number of records.
