About this data

Source: Canada — Calgary building permits. Published by The City of Calgary, Open Calgary, updated daily (publisher). Licence: City of Calgary open data terms of use. Values are the amounts declared on the application, not final costs.

  • Calgary only; not nationwide Canada.
  • Permits as published by the city. Coverage, field meanings and update delays follow the publisher.
  • Project values are the amounts declared to the city, in Canadian dollars, not final construction costs.

Questions

How often is Calgary permit data updated?

The City of Calgary, Open Calgary updates the open data daily (publisher). A run always reads the latest published records.

Do the building permits include contractor names?

Yes, for 43% of building permits in this period, as published by the city.
