NSW development applications

At least 7,536 development applications were lodged in New South Wales in the four weeks from 28 September to 25 October 2026, at least 1,884 a week. This page sums them up from the official open data (State of New South Wales and Department of Planning, Housing and Infrastructure) and shows how to get the full list, record by record. Some weeks go past our sample of 2,000 development applications, so the counts on this page are a floor.

Data checked 28 October 2026. Source: State of New South Wales and Department of Planning, Housing and Infrastructure.
