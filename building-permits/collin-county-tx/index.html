About this data

Source: United States — Collin County (Texas) building permits. Published by Collin Central Appraisal District, via the Texas Open Data Portal, updated daily (publisher). Licence: Texas Open Data Portal terms of use. Values are the amounts declared on the application, not final costs.

  • Collin County, Texas only; not nationwide United States.
  • Issued permits as published by the city. Coverage, field meanings and update delays follow the publisher.
  • Project values are the amounts declared to the city, not final construction costs.
