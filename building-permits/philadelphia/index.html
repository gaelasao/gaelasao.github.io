About this data

Source: United States — Philadelphia permits (L&I). Published by City of Philadelphia, Department of Licenses and Inspections, via OpenDataPhilly, updated daily (publisher). Licence: City of Philadelphia open data terms of use. Values are the amounts declared on the application, not final costs.

  • Philadelphia only; not nationwide United States.
  • Issued permits as published by the city. Coverage, field meanings and update delays follow the publisher.
  • Project values are the amounts declared to the city, not final construction costs.
