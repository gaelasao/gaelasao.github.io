About this data

Source: United States — Los Angeles building permits (LADBS). Published by City of Los Angeles, LADBS, via Los Angeles Open Data, updated daily (publisher). Licence: Los Angeles Open Data terms of use. Values are the amounts declared on the application, not final costs.

  • Los Angeles only; not nationwide United States.
  • Issued permits as published by the city. Coverage, field meanings and update delays follow the publisher.
  • Project values are the amounts declared to the city, not final construction costs.

Questions

How often is Los Angeles permit data updated?

City of Los Angeles, LADBS, via Los Angeles Open Data updates the open data daily (publisher). A run always reads the latest published records.

Yes. Filter by date range, ZIP code, keyword in the description or type, and cap the number of records.
