About this data

Source: United States — New Orleans permits. Published by City of New Orleans, Department of Safety and Permits, updated daily (publisher). Licence: CC0 1.0 Universal (public domain dedication). Values are the amounts declared on the application, not final costs.

  • New Orleans only; not nationwide United States.
  • Issued permits as published by the city. Coverage, field meanings and update delays follow the publisher.
  • Project values are the amounts declared to the city, not final construction costs.
