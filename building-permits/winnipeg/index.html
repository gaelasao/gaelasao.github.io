About this data

Source: Canada — Winnipeg building permits. Published by City of Winnipeg Open Data, updated monthly (publisher). Licence: City of Winnipeg open data terms of use. Values are the amounts declared on the application, not final costs.

  • Winnipeg only; not nationwide Canada.
  • Permits as published by the city. Coverage, field meanings and update delays follow the publisher.
  • Project values are the amounts declared to the city, in Canadian dollars, not final construction costs.

Questions

How often is Winnipeg permit data updated?

City of Winnipeg Open Data updates the open data monthly (publisher). A run always reads the latest published records.

Do the building permits include contractor names?

Yes, for 40% of building permits in this period, as published by the city.

Can I get only some building permits?

Yes. Filter by date range, keyword in the description or type, and cap the number of records.
