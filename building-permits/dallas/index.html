About this data

Source: United States — Dallas building permits. Published by City of Dallas, Dallas OpenData, updated daily (publisher). Licence: Dallas OpenData terms of use. Values are the amounts declared on the application, not final costs.

  • Dallas only; not nationwide United States.
  • Issued permits as published by the city. Coverage, field meanings and update delays follow the publisher.
  • Project values are the amounts declared to the city, not final construction costs.

Questions

How often is Dallas permit data updated?

City of Dallas, Dallas OpenData updates the open data daily (publisher). A run always reads the latest published records.

Can I get only some building permits?

Yes. Filter by date range, ZIP code, keyword in the description or type, and cap the number of records.
