About this data

Source: United States — Boston approved building permits. Published by City of Boston, Inspectional Services Department, via Analyze Boston, updated daily (publisher). Licence: Open Data Commons Public Domain Dedication and License (PDDL). Values are the amounts declared on the application, not final costs.

  • Boston only; not nationwide United States.
  • Issued permits as published by the city. Coverage, field meanings and update delays follow the publisher.
  • Project values are the amounts declared to the city, not final construction costs.

Questions

How often is Boston permit data updated?

City of Boston, Inspectional Services Department, via Analyze Boston updates the open data daily (publisher). A run always reads the latest published records.

Yes. Filter by date range, ZIP code, keyword in the description or type, and cap the number of records.
