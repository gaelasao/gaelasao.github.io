About this data

Source: United States — Baton Rouge (East Baton Rouge Parish) building permits. Published by City of Baton Rouge / Parish of East Baton Rouge, Open Data BR, updated daily (publisher). Licence: Public Domain (publisher declaration). Values are the amounts declared on the application, not final costs.

  • East Baton Rouge Parish only; not nationwide United States.
  • Issued permits as published by the city. Coverage, field meanings and update delays follow the publisher.
  • Project values are the amounts declared to the city, not final construction costs.
