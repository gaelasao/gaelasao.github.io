About this data

Source: Canada — Edmonton building permits. Published by City of Edmonton Open Data, updated daily (publisher). Licence: City of Edmonton open data terms of use. Values are the amounts declared on the application, not final costs.

  • Edmonton only; not nationwide Canada.
  • Permits as published by the city. Coverage, field meanings and update delays follow the publisher.
  • Project values are the amounts declared to the city, in Canadian dollars, not final construction costs.

City of Edmonton Open Data updates the open data daily (publisher). A run always reads the latest published records.
