About this data

Source: United States — New York City DOB NOW approved permits. Published by NYC Department of Buildings (DOB), NYC Open Data, updated daily (publisher). Licence: NYC Open Data Terms of Use. Values are the amounts declared on the application, not final costs.

  • New York City only; not nationwide United States.
  • Issued permits as published by the city. Coverage, field meanings and update delays follow the publisher.
  • Project values are the amounts declared to the city, not final construction costs.

Questions

How often is New York City permit data updated?

NYC Department of Buildings (DOB), NYC Open Data updates the open data daily (publisher). A run always reads the latest published records.

Do the building permits include contractor names?

Yes, for 100% of building permits in this period, as published by the city.
