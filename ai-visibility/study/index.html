Which brands do AI assistants recommend?

On 26 October 2026 we asked four AI engines (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini and Google AI Overviews) 50 typical buying questions, such as “best CRM for small business” or “best VPN for streaming”, in 10 categories. That made 200 checks and 198 answers. In 5 of the 10 categories, the AI answers named a challenger more often than the best-known brand.

Study run with the AI Visibility Tracker, US results, English questions.

Where the big names lose

  • Email marketing: MailerLite is named in 79% of answers, Mailchimp in 42%.
  • Password managers: Bitwarden is named in 90% of answers, 1Password in 85%.
  • Running shoes: Hoka is named in 79% of answers, Nike in 68%.
  • VPNs: Proton VPN is named in 100% of answers, NordVPN in 80%.
  • Web hosting: Hostinger is named in 100% of answers, Bluehost in 60%.

Averages over the 10 categories. An answer can name a brand without linking to its website: being cited as a source is rarer than being named. Gemini listed sources in every answer, but only review and comparison sites: it never cited the brand’s own website in this sample.

Method and raw data

In each category we tracked one well-known brand and its main rivals. Each question was asked once per engine, in the United States, on 26 October 2026. A brand counts as named when it appears in the answer text, and as cited when one of its domains is among the answer’s sources. AI answers change from day to day, so treat this as a snapshot.
