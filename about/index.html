About

I’m Gaël Gerard, an independent developer based in France. I build small data tools and publish them on the Apify Store, where people pay per result.

The three tools on this site are the Building Permits API, the Keyword Research API and the AI Visibility Tracker. The pages here show real data from each, refreshed when I update the site.

Disclosure

I earn money when these tools are used. Links to Apify go to my tools. I am not affiliated with Apify, with the cities whose open data I use, or with the brands in the AI study.
