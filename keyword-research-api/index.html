A keyword research API without a Google Ads account

Google Keyword Planner shows vague ranges unless you run ads. This API returns exact monthly search volume, cost per click, difficulty from 0 to 100, search intent, AI search volume and the features on the results page, for up to thousands of keywords per run.

“AI searches” estimates how often the topic comes up in AI assistants. Difficulty runs from 0 (easy) to 100 (hard).

Three ways to use it

  • Metrics: paste a list of keywords, get volume, CPC, difficulty, intent, 12-month trend and SERP features for each.
  • Ideas: give a seed keyword, get related keywords, long-tail suggestions or Google’s related searches, with the same metrics.
  • Competitors: give up to 10 domains, get the keywords each one ranks for, with position and estimated traffic.

What to target first

Every run ends with keyword opportunities picked from its own rows, at no extra cost: low-difficulty keywords, buying-intent keywords ranked by searches × CPC, questions to answer, keywords also asked in AI assistants, rising and seasonal keywords, SERP features to win and, for a website, the keywords ranking 4 to 20.

Questions

Where does the data come from?

From DataForSEO’s Google Ads and Labs data, the same sources many SEO tools resell. The tool adds clean fields and filters.

Which countries are supported?

All countries that Google Ads covers. Difficulty, intent and trend come with the 94 countries of DataForSEO Labs. Bing volume is included on runs of 300 keywords or more, in English, French and German.

How does it pick the keywords to target first?

With fixed rules on the rows of the run: a difficulty of 30 or less, commercial or transactional intent with a CPC, questions, AI search volume, growth over the year, SERP features and, for a website, positions 4 to 20. Nothing extra is charged.

What is keyword difficulty?

An estimate from 0 to 100 of how hard it is to reach the first page of Google for a keyword, based on the pages that already rank there. It is a starting point: a difficulty of 30 is easier, not easy for every site.

What is AI search volume?

DataForSEO’s estimate of how often a keyword comes up in AI assistants such as ChatGPT, built from People Also Ask data. It is included at no extra cost.

Can AI agents use it?

Yes. It is available through the Apify MCP server, so Claude, ChatGPT or Cursor can call it as a tool.
